# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Client Network Ltd, trading as Client Network ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our websites or interact with our services.

## Information We Collect

- **Information you provide:** name, email address, phone number, company details, and any information you submit through our intake and booking forms.
- **Automatically collected information:** device type, browser, pages visited, and referring URLs, collected through standard web technologies.

## How We Use Information

- To respond to enquiries and schedule strategy calls.
- To provide and improve our lead generation services.
- To send service communications you have requested.
- To meet legal and compliance obligations, including consent record-keeping under the TCPA.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as CRM and scheduling tools), and where required by law. Consumer leads generated through our advertising are delivered exclusively to the single client who purchased that campaign's production.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide services and to meet legal, accounting, and compliance requirements, then delete or anonymise it.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email us at <hello@clientnetwork.io>.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary to run this site and understand aggregate usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Our Meta Advertising Tools

We operate a private Meta (Facebook) app used only by our own team to manage our own advertising through Meta's Marketing API. It is not offered to the public and does not use Facebook Login for members of the public.

- **What it accesses:** ad account, campaign, ad set, ad, creative and performance data, and details of the Facebook Pages and Business Managers we own or are authorised to manage.
- **What it does not collect:** personal data about Facebook or Instagram users. Performance data is aggregated and contains no individual user information.
- **How we use it:** only to create, manage and report on our own advertising. We do not sell this data, share it with third parties, or use it on other advertising platforms.
- **Retention:** data is stored securely on our own systems and deleted when it is no longer needed for managing our advertising.
- **Data deletion:** to request deletion of any data our app holds, email <hello@clientnetwork.io> with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
